# Amendment A5 (frozen 2026-08-11, prior to any run it governs)

Purpose: (i) to replicate the interface comparison in a current-generation
model family (reviewer: baseline staleness), (ii) to add the fine-tuned VLM
baseline (reviewer M1), and (iii) to complete the confirmatory-bank audit
with a blind arm.

## New arms

Contemporary-family arms (transformers, greedy, max_new_tokens = 8, frozen
parse_letter, prompts byte-identical to the A3 arms; documented deviation:
HuggingFace chat templates instead of ollama's):
- pixel: Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct and Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct on the confirmatory
  bank (bf16; the ollama route is rejected on evidence: its qwen3-vl builds
  ignore think:false and emit nothing within an 8-token budget);
- text: Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 and Qwen3-8B reading D0 at the confirmatory
  bank. Qwen3-8B has no 2507-Instruct variant and is run in its official
  non-thinking mode (enable_thinking=False in the chat template); recorded
  as a template-level note, not a decoding change.

Fine-tuned VLM baseline (M1):
- Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct, one LoRA adapter per fold of the existing
  4-fold sequence rotation; training QA generated by the FROZEN
  make_confirm_qa.py run unmodified over the fold's training-sequence
  frames (all confirmatory frames excluded from every training set);
  SFT target is the gold letter under the same pixel-arm prompt.
  Out-of-fold inference on the confirmatory bank, same frames, same items,
  same parse. Hyperparameters are selected only on a validation sequence
  carved out of fold-0's training sequences; dev and confirmatory banks are
  never touched for selection.
- Qwen2.5-VL-7B via QLoRA if a 100-step feasibility smoke passes on this
  machine; otherwise reported as 3B-only with the failure reason.

Audit arm:
- blind (question + options, no scene) at qwen2.5:3b and 7b on the
  confirmatory bank, closing the gap that the only existing blind runs
  cover the development bank.

## Deviation-recording rule
Every departure from the A3 protocol (template family, thinking budgets,
quantization) is recorded next to the number it produced; arms with a
favorable asymmetry (recorded-deviation thinking arm) remain descriptive
and enter no paired contrast.

## Pre-stated expectation (M1)
After fine-tuning, Delta(D0 - PIXEL_ft) narrows relative to the zero-shot
Delta(D0 - PIXEL). Whatever the direction and size of the remainder, all
outcomes are reported, and headline wording will state the system-level
comparison (zero-shot deployment) and the supervision-matched comparison
(fine-tuned) as separate claims.

All outcomes will be reported regardless of direction. Nothing frozen under
A3 or A4 is modified.
